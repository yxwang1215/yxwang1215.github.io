# Paper page assets

`papers/assets/` contains local figures and paper PDFs used by the paper project pages.

- `srp-sft-intro.png` is the SRP-SFT objective comparison shown near the top of the page.
- `srp-sft-method.png` is the one-position method overview from the paper.
- `srp-sft-mechanism.png` is the held-out geometry diagnostic figure.
- `srp-sft-paper.pdf` is the archived arXiv:2610.19685v1 paper PDF.
- `glance-etd-overview.png`, `glance-etd-motivation.png`, `glance-etd-budget.png`, `glance-etd-gemma-budget.png`, and `glance-etd-efficiency.png` are the overview, motivation, budget, and efficiency figures used by the Glance-ETD project page.
- `glance-etd-paper.pdf` is the supplied anonymous CVPR 2027 review-copy PDF.

Large model weights, caches, and training checkpoints stay in the research workspace and are not copied here.
