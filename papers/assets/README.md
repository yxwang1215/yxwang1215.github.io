# Paper page assets

`papers/assets/` contains local figures and paper PDFs used by the paper project pages.

- `lp-sft-motivation.png` is the diagnostic figure shown on the LP-SFT page.
- `lp-sft-paper.pdf` is the archived LP-SFT paper PDF.
- `lp-sft-reproduction.pdf` is the related double-column reproduction report used as a visual and evidence reference.

Large model weights, caches, and training checkpoints stay in the research workspace and are not copied here.
