Abstract

Spend vision compute where it matters, then keep the evidence that answers the question.

At a strict 5% visual-token budget, a multimodal LLM still pays the cost of encoding every image patch, and the few surviving tokens can miss the answer. Glance-ETD uses a two-stage pipeline: a glance encoder reads a quarter-resolution image, chooses diverse cells, and re-encodes only those regions at native resolution; Evidence-Tempered Diversity (ETD) then tilts max–min selection toward a supervised evidence map. In the supplied review copy, the pipeline reaches 90.3±0.8% retention of the full-token score, while a dense encoder plus ETD reaches 91.7%, and the glance encoder reduces HR-Bench 4K prefill from 373 to 65 TFLOPs.

01

Method

A glance decides where to look; ETD decides what to keep.

The model is trained with on-policy self-distillation and DivPrune, then evaluated with the evidence-tempered selector at the same token budget.

A

Stage 1 · Glance encoder

Average-pool the image into a coarse grid, select 25% of cells by max–min diversity, and re-encode those cells at native resolution. The merged grid keeps the dense encoder’s positions at about one sixth of its FLOPs.

B

Stage 2 · Evidence scorer

A 1.9M-parameter scorer reads image tokens and the question, producing a relative evidence map p and a text confidence t from box supervision.

C

Evidence-Tempered Diversity

ETD keeps the diversity gain dᵢ while weighting candidates by relative evidence: i⋆ = arg max dᵢ · (Npᵢ)βt. With flat evidence it becomes DivPrune; with text evidence it protects the answer region without giving up context.

03

Efficiency

The glance path moves the bottleneck before token pruning.

On HR-Bench 4K, Glance-ETD reports a 2.9× prefill speed-up and 1.4 points less retention than the dense encoder plus ETD.

04

What to remember

Three choices make extreme compression usable.

01

Look cheaply first

A coarse pass chooses a small set of regions before native-resolution vision work begins.

02

Keep diversity in the loop

Evidence moves the selector toward text and answer regions, while max–min distance preserves coverage for scene questions.

03

Use one budget everywhere

All selectors compare the same strict K = ⌈0.05N⌉ tokens per image, so the difference comes from which tokens survive.
