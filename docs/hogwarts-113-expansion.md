The 13 new discoveries require an explicit **inspect → revealed opening → enter** sequence. The original Chamber of Secrets tap and Room of Requirement three-pass entrance are unchanged. The public Atlas is read-only and never grants a session visit. All new scene boards are unique, self-contained 1600 × 900 SVG concept illustrations; they are deliberately not presented as photographic or canon-accurate artwork. Several activation points added to the original photographic scenes are provisional and should receive manual visual placement before a final art pass.

The Hog's Head-to-Humpbacked-Witch route is a **site-specific invented route**, not a canon claim. The One-Eyed Witch route is modeled with separate concealed approaches from the second-floor corridor and Honeydukes cellar. The four expanded Rooms of Requirement are interpretive views, not simultaneous stable canonical subdivisions.
