      02 / WALK THE WORLD

      Passage Rules

      Find a visible passage, choose its opening and begin walking.

      1. 01

        Walk Through Openings, Not Objects

        Passages follow visible doors, arches, stairs, hatches and paths. Point at an opening to discover its destination; tables, pumpkins, railings and open water are not exits. To return, follow the marked way back.

      2. 02

        Reveal a Secret Before Entering

        A secret mechanism reveals an entrance but does not teleport you. After the doorway or pipe is visible, click it to enter. Opened entrances remain revealed during the same journey.

      3. 03

        Choose, Then Confirm, with the Keyboard

        Use Tab to focus a passage, or the arrow keys and WASD to choose a direction. Press Enter or Space to confirm. Holding a key will not carry you through multiple scenes.

      4. 04

        Pinch to Inspect on Mobile

        The entire scene remains visible and passage regions scale with the image. Pinch to inspect small doors or distant paths. Swiping does not trigger travel; explicitly tap the next opening.

      03 / BOOKS, PASSAGES & INTERPRETATION

      What Comes from the Books?

      Book references take priority; uncertain connections are labelled as reconstructions.

      The seven novels were cross-checked against chapter indexes and official references. A place appearing in the books does not mean every neighbouring door on this map is canonical.Evidence is annotated separately for places and connections. Where the books do not specify a passage, a clearly marked reconstruction fills the gap.

      British floor terminology is used: Ground floor is the entrance level; First floor is one level above. Some legacy URL segments retain earlier floor labels for compatibility; the annotations here describe the adopted geography.

      I

      The Kitchens Lie Beneath the Great Hall

      Entrance Hall → descending stairs → basement corridor → opened fruit portrait → kitchens. There is no invented side door directly through the Great Hall.

      Goblet of Fire, ch. 21 · Chapter index ↗
      II

      Classrooms and Offices Are Destinations

      Shared corridors, landings and tower stairs reach these rooms. You no longer pass through Potions to reach the reservoir or through the Headmaster's Office to reach the Owlery, which sits atop the West Tower.

      Owlery in the West Tower · Official reference ↗
      III

      Divination: Up Through the Trapdoor

      North Tower landing → silver ladder → trapdoor → Divination classroom. The return also uses the floor opening; there is no direct shortcut to the Astronomy Tower.

      Prisoner of Azkaban, ch. 6 · Chapter index ↗
      IV

      Hagrid's Hut Stands Beside the Forest

      Reach Hagrid's hut by a school path, then choose whether to explore the Forbidden Forest or paddock. The scene shows the hut; passing through the forest is no longer required.

      Hagrid's Hut · Official reference ↗
      Notes on Hidden Entrances Contains story and route spoilers

      Seventh Floor: The Door Opposite the Tapestry

      The Room of Requirement is on the seventh floor. Walk past the blank wall three times while concentrating on a need to make the door appear. The site condenses this into a reveal action; entering requires a separate click.

      Official reference · Order of the Phoenix, ch. 18 ↗

      Bathroom: Reveal the Pipe First

      The serpent-marked tap is a trigger, not a teleport control. The opening appears in a separate view before it can be entered. The upward return draws on Fawkes's assistance; the far end of the Chamber is another view of the same room.

      Chamber of Secrets, chs. 16–17 · Chapter index ↗

      Where the Books Leave Questions

      The Hospital Wing and Headmaster's Office are approached from different floors in different books, while the precise floor of Transfiguration is not fixed. This map makes practical choices and explains them at the relevant locations. The viaduct, cloisters, storerooms and reservoir are identified as reconstructions or original additions, not novel-established names.
