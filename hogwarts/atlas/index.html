      II. HOW TO WANDER

      Rules of Passage

      A doorway should be entered, not merely imagined.

      1. I

        Follow a real opening

        Inside the scenes, doors, arches, stairs, trapdoors and paths are the exits. Selecting furniture or scenery does not move you to another room.

      2. II

        Reveal the hidden entrance

        A concealed passage may require a small discovery first. Uncover the opening, then select it again to pass through. Previously opened secrets remain visible in the journey.

      3. III

        Wander with the keyboard

        Use Tab to focus a passage or use the arrow keys and WASD to select one. Press Enter to take it.

      4. IV

        Take the map with you

        On a small screen, scroll and zoom to inspect narrow passages. In the explorable scenes, the parchment map remembers the rooms you have visited.

      III. BETWEEN CANON & CARTOGRAPHY

      Notes from the Mapmakers

      Where the books are clear, the passages follow. Elsewhere, the reconstruction is marked.

      These locations and their connections were checked against chapter-indexed references and official material. A place appearing in the novels does not prove that every neighbouring doorway is described there. Added passages are marked as reconstructions rather than presented as canon.

      Floor labels follow British usage: the ground floor is the entrance level and the first floor is above it. Some historical URL names are retained for compatibility, even where the reconstruction uses a different floor.

      I

      Below the Great Hall

      Entrance Hall → descending stairs → basement passage → fruit-painted doorway → kitchens. There is no invented shortcut through a side door of the Great Hall.

      Goblet of Fire, Chapter 21 · Chapter index ↗
      II

      Rooms are destinations

      Classrooms and offices are reached from corridors and landings, not used as implausible through-passages. The Owlery remains at the top of the West Tower.

      The Owlery · Official reference ↗
      III

      Through the trapdoor

      North Tower landing → ladder → trapdoor → Divination Classroom. Return through the floor opening rather than inventing a direct passage to the Astronomy Tower.

      Prisoner of Azkaban, Chapter 6 ↗
      IV

      At the forest's edge

      Hagrid's Hut can be reached from the school grounds before choosing to explore the Forbidden Forest or the nearby paddocks.

      Hagrid's Hut · Official reference ↗
      Notes on hidden entrances Contains story and passage spoilers

      The seventh-floor wall

      The Room of Requirement appears opposite the tapestry after three deliberate passes with a particular need in mind. The site condenses this into a reveal interaction; entering remains a separate action.

      Official reference · Order of the Phoenix, Chapter 18 ↗

      The serpent-marked tap

      The tap in Myrtle's bathroom is a mechanism, not a teleport button. First expose the pipe, then enter it. The upward return draws on Fawkes's assistance; the chamber's far end is another view of the same space.

      Chamber of Secrets, Chapters 16–17 ↗

      The map is not infallible

      The Hospital Wing and Headmaster's Office have ambiguous floor descriptions across the books; the Transfiguration classroom has no fixed canonical floor. These are arranged plausibly here. Added bridges, cloisters, storage rooms and reservoirs are creative reconstructions, not independently established book locations.
