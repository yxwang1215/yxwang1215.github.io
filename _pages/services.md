# Work With Me
{: style="margin-top: 0.8em; margin-bottom: 10px;"}
<div class="section-divider"></div>

I take on a small number of focused projects where a concrete systems or evaluation problem can be solved within a clearly defined scope. My work centers on efficient LLM inference, KV-cache optimization, multimodal systems, and Agentic Coding data and evaluation.

## 01 · LLM Inference & Serving Audit

For research groups and engineering teams working on latency, memory, throughput, or long-context inference.

**Typical scope**

- TTFT, inter-token latency, throughput, batching, and scheduling diagnosis
- KV-cache allocation, eviction, compression, reuse, and CPU–GPU transfer
- Audio, multimodal, long-context, and multi-model serving workloads
- Benchmark design, profiling methodology, and bottleneck attribution

**Engagement formats**

- **Focused diagnostic** — 60 minutes, followed by a concise action memo; **from ¥499 / US$79**
- **Written systems audit** — profiling plan, bottleneck map, and prioritized fixes; **from ¥2,999 / US$399**
- **Short PoC sprint** — implementation and before/after evaluation; **from ¥9,800 / US$1,500**

## 02 · Agentic Coding Data & Evaluation Review

For teams building SFT data, coding agents, harnesses, verifiers, or security-oriented evaluations.

**Typical scope**

- Training-data provenance, trajectory validity, and tool-result integrity
- Harness and protocol compatibility across training and deployment
- Verifier, regression-test, and benchmark design
- Failure taxonomy, credit assignment, and data-quality filtering
- Security coding evaluation and reproducible experiment pipelines

**Engagement formats**

- **Dataset or benchmark review** — 90 minutes with written findings; **from ¥699 / US$99**
- **Full audit** — sampled trajectory inspection, risk map, and remediation plan; **from ¥3,999 / US$599**
- **Pipeline sprint** — audit scripts, verifier design, or evaluation integration; **from ¥12,800 / US$1,800**

## 03 · Research Engineering Review

For students and research teams that already have a project and need rigorous technical feedback rather than ghostwriting.

**Typical scope**

- Method and experiment design review
- Reproducibility, ablation, baseline, and systems-metric checks
- Code organization, evaluation scripts, and release readiness
- Presentation, project page, and technical narrative feedback

**Engagement formats**

- **45-minute project clinic** — **from ¥399 / US$59**
- **Deep technical review** — code, draft, and experiment plan; **from ¥1,999 / US$299**

I do not write assignments, fabricate results, or provide authorship-for-payment services.

## 04 · Technical Workshops

Custom 60–90 minute sessions for laboratories, student groups, and engineering teams.

Available topics include KV-cache systems, efficient multimodal inference, diffusion LLM acceleration, Agentic Coding data pipelines, and benchmark design. Workshops start at **¥2,500 / US$399**.

## How a Project Starts

1. Send a brief description of the system, current bottleneck, available artifacts, and desired outcome.
2. I reply with a fit assessment and a fixed scope. A short fit check is free.
3. We agree on deliverables, timeline, confidentiality, and payment before work begins.
4. The project ends with reusable artifacts: a memo, benchmark, patch, script, or implementation—not only a call.
